About the AI Moderator

Every report is checked against an AI model (via a Supabase Edge Function) the moment it's submitted. Anything flagged as spam/test or too vague stays hidden from the public map and reports page until an admin approves it here. Reports are also cross-checked against recent submissions for likely duplicates using a rule-based building + category + timing match. Confidence scores reflect the model's own certainty in its classification.
